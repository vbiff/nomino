Nomino

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Nomino: Card Scanner (“Nomino”, “the app”) handles your information. Nomino is developed by Mikhail Bogovalov.

In short: Nomino does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything you scan or enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The contacts you save, your own card, events, notes, photos of scanned cards and the text recognized from them are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. The developer has no access to this data.

This data is part of your regular iPhone backup (for example iCloud Backup), which is managed by Apple under your Apple Account settings, not by Nomino.

Camera, Photos and Contacts

  • Camera – used only while you scan a business card, badge or QR code. Images are processed on the device.
  • Photos – when you import an image, Nomino receives only the photo you pick, not your library.
  • Contacts – when you import from iPhone Contacts, Nomino receives only the contact you pick. It does not read your address book.

On-device processing

Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework. On supported devices, field parsing may use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). This processing happens on your iPhone; Nomino does not send your data to any server.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Nomino has no sign-in, contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing you initiate

When you export contacts to CSV or share your card as a .vcf file, the file is passed to the app or service you choose (for example Mail or Telegram). What happens to it there is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual contacts and events in the app. Deleting Nomino from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Nomino is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
