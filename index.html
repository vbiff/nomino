Nomino

Collect contacts at every event

Nomino turns business cards, event badges and QR codes into contacts you can search, tag by event and export to CSV. Everything is processed on your iPhone.

Scan anything people hand you
  • Paper business cards and conference badges
  • Contact QR codes (vCard, MeCard) and LinkedIn links
  • Contacts shared to your iPhone via NameDrop or AirDrop
Private by design

Nomino has no account, no servers and no analytics. Your contacts stay on your device.
